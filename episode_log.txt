EP001 — The Guard She Underestimated
  Logline: A stoic young bodyguard who is humiliated by the wealthy teen he protects must save her life hours later, forcing her to confront who actually deserves her respect.
  Creator insight: The people we're quickest to look down on are often the ones who show up hardest when it counts, and real respect is earned in a crisis, not given by status.
  Tema AS: LA private-security/bodyguard-for-hire culture (Leo's job stakes drive the plot); industrial back-alley setting as escalation ground.
  PLOT DNA: 1) motor konflik: kewajiban tugas vs penghinaan pribadi | 2) relasi: pengawal-klien (bodyguard-employer) | 3) titik balik: Brutus mengadang dan mengancam Chloe di gang | 4) resolusi: kemenangan kecil, rekonsiliasi hormat (non-romantis) | 5) nada: tense-relief | 6) hook: aksi mendadak (Chloe menjatuhkan air ke sepatu Leo)
  Soft-limit elements used: konflik fisik (S02-S03, tanpa darah/luka, R10 aktif).
  Karakter berulang (jika serial): none (episode standalone).


EP001 — The Photo in Her Wallet
  Logline: A rule-bound credit union teller wants to keep the system fair for everyone, but a panicked mother begs for an emergency withdrawal outside system limits, forcing him to choose between blind compliance and finding a legitimate way to help.
  Creator insight: Systems built to protect people from fraud are the same systems that can make someone truly in need invisible; compliance and compassion are not automatically opposed, but someone has to choose to hold both at once.
  Tema AS: community credit union with a strict fraud-hold verification policy driving the plot; medical/ER urgency as the reason for the emergency request (no financial figures or advice given to viewers).
  PLOT DNA: 1) motor konflik: aturan/sistem vs waktu | 2) relasi: orang asing (teller-nasabah) | 3) titik balik: teller melihat foto anak nasabah yang lusuh jatuh dari dompet, mengingatkannya pada alasan pribadinya sendiri | 4) resolusi: kemenangan kecil lewat eskalasi manajer yang sah, bukan pelanggaran aturan | 5) nada: quiet-drama, tense-relief | 6) hook: layar sistem "TRANSACTION DENIED" muncul bersamaan nasabah memukul meja panik
  Soft-limit elements used: kesulitan uang (medical/ER urgency) — motor cerita, resolusi manusiawi, tanpa angka/nasihat keuangan ke penonton.
  Karakter berulang: none (episode standalone).
