EP017 — The Window He Broke To Save A Stranger
  Logline: A window washer working extra shifts to pay for his daughter's treatment gets trapped on a collapsing platform 78 floors up when a storm hits, and a hedge fund executive must decide whether his own glass office is worth more than a stranger's life.
  Creator insight: The people who look coldest on paper are sometimes the fastest to destroy their own wealth the moment a life is actually on the line; kindness under real pressure says more than kindness performed for an audience.
  Tema AS: high-rise hedge fund/corporate office culture (bulletproof glass becomes both barrier and rescue tool); high-rise window-washing gig labor (medical-cost backstory drives why Marcus keeps working through the storm).
  PLOT DNA: 1) motor konflik: bahaya fisik mendadak akibat badai, bukan uang/relasi/rahasia | 2) relasi: orang asing total | 3) titik balik: tali pengaman putus total, satu-satunya jalan adalah eksekutif memecahkan kaca anti-pelurunya sendiri | 4) resolusi: penyelamatan fisik langsung lewat pengorbanan properti pribadi | 5) nada: tense-relief menuju quiet-warm | 6) hook: aksi fisik mendadak (angin badai, alat terlempar, tali terkelupas)
  Soft-limit elements used: kesulitan biaya pengobatan anak (motor motivasi, tanpa angka/nasihat); bahaya fisik/kontak fisik ringan akibat kecelakaan dan penyelamatan (S04-S05, tanpa cedera terlihat, R10 aktif).
  Karakter berulang (jika serial): none.

EP016 — Her Grocery Card Declined—Then the Cashier Did This Quietly
  Logline: A grocery cashier sees a tired customer's card decline and quietly uses a customer-funded Neighbor Credit system instead of letting her leave hungry or humiliated.
  Creator insight: Real kindness protects dignity instead of performing charity; one quiet decision at an American grocery checkout can turn hardship into a shared community habit.
  Tema AS: grocery checkout culture; food insecurity; customer-funded community assistance.
  PLOT DNA: 1) motor konflik: kartu belanja ditolak dan rasa malu publik | 2) relasi: cashier-customer menuju customer-community | 3) titik balik: ledger membuktikan dana berasal dari pelanggan biasa | 4) resolusi: dignity restored dan sistem bantuan dibuat permanen | 5) nada: quiet-tension menuju warm-relief | 6) hook: terminal PAYMENT DECLINED lalu groceries dikembalikan.
  Soft-limit elements used: kesulitan ekonomi sebagai konteks manusiawi, tanpa angka atau nasihat.
  Karakter berulang: Blythe Hargrove, Juniper Cole, Gage Whitlock.

EP015 — He Chased a Thief, and Found Someone to Save
  Logline: A hot-tempered bakery owner wants to catch and punish the stray dog that stole from his shop, but cornering it in a dead-end alley forces him to see who the dog was really stealing for, and he must decide whether his anger is worth more than a stranger's life.
  Creator insight: A stray dog and a starving kid had already built their own working system of survival, sharing food every single day, long before the man with the store and the money ever noticed or thought to help.
  Tema AS: independent bakery ownership culture (gives the job offer personal weight); urban winter homelessness and hypothermia risk (drives the medical emergency).
  PLOT DNA: 1) motor konflik: pencurian (dimediasi hewan) yang berbalik jadi rasa bersalah & tanggung jawab | 2) relasi: segitiga manusia-hewan-manusia (pemilik toko - anjing liar - pemuda tunawisma) | 3) titik balik: pemilik toko melihat anjing meletakkan roti curian di pangkuan pemuda yang menggigil, sadar ini rutinitas harian | 4) resolusi: biaya perawatan ditanggung + pekerjaan di toko roti + anjing diadopsi jadi maskot | 5) nada: bittersweet ke heartwarming | 6) hook: aksi fisik mendadak (pencurian roti + kejaran pakai sapu)
  Soft-limit elements used: homelessness + hypothermia emergency (resolved humanely, no medical figures/advice); brief non-violent physical contact (S04, R10 applied).
  Karakter berulang (jika serial): none (episode standalone).

EP001 — The Photo in Her Wallet
  Logline: A rule-bound credit union teller wants to keep the system fair for everyone, but a panicked mother begs for an emergency withdrawal outside system limits, forcing him to choose between blind compliance and finding a legitimate way to help.
  Creator insight: Systems built to protect people from fraud are the same systems that can make someone truly in need invisible; compliance and compassion are not automatically opposed, but someone has to choose to hold both at once.
  Tema AS: community credit union with a strict fraud-hold verification policy driving the plot; medical/ER urgency as the reason for the emergency request (no financial figures or advice given to viewers).
  PLOT DNA: 1) motor konflik: aturan/sistem vs waktu | 2) relasi: orang asing (teller-nasabah) | 3) titik balik: teller melihat foto anak nasabah yang lusuh jatuh dari dompet, mengingatkannya pada alasan pribadinya sendiri | 4) resolusi: kemenangan kecil lewat eskalasi manajer yang sah, bukan pelanggaran aturan | 5) nada: quiet-drama, tense-relief | 6) hook: layar sistem "TRANSACTION DENIED" muncul bersamaan nasabah memukul meja panik
  Soft-limit elements used: kesulitan uang (medical/ER urgency) — motor cerita, resolusi manusiawi, tanpa angka/nasihat keuangan ke penonton.
  Karakter berulang: none (episode standalone).
