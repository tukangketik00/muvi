EP026 — The Pie She Kept Alive
  Logline: Walt Hendricks, a pie shop owner who has stopped believing he can bake anything worth entering, doesn't know his daughter has secretly signed him up for the county fair using his late wife's recipes, and he must decide whether to pick up the legacy she left behind or let the entry go to waste.
  Creator insight: A recipe left in a box isn't a memory yet — it only becomes one when someone's hands make it again.
  Tema AS: County Fair Baking Competition drives the plot through a secret registration and public judging; the family-run pie shop is both setting and stakes.
  PLOT DNA: 1) motor konflik: rahasia (pendaftaran diam-diam) | 2) relasi: ayah-anak | 3) titik balik: Walt menemukan buku catatan Ruthie berisi percobaan resep ibunya yang gagal berulang kali, terselip di balik formulir pendaftaran | 4) resolusi: rekonsiliasi + kemenangan kecil (membuka kembali toko dengan nama resep ibunya) | 5) nada: bittersweet-warm | 6) hook: aksi visual mendadak + dialog tajam (Walt membuang pai gagal sambil bergumam bukan pembuat pai kompetisi)
  Soft-limit elements used: none.
  Karakter berulang (jika serial): none (episode standalone).
  Summary: Ruthie diam-diam mendaftarkan ayahnya, Walt, ke kompetisi memanggang county fair memakai resep mendiang ibunya, dan setelah amarah Walt luruh saat menemukan catatan percobaan Ruthie yang gagal berulang kali, mereka membuat ulang resep itu bersama dan Walt membuka kembali toko pai dengan nama resep ibunya terpampang.

EP025 — The Test She Never Agreed To
  Logline: Wyatt Calloway, heir to a family firm who spent eight months posing as a day-rate handyman, wants to propose to Nora Copeland after proving she loves him without knowing his wealth, but his identity is exposed first in the diner's back parking lane, and he must accept that the secret test he ran on her destroyed the very thing he wanted to prove.
  Creator insight: American media sells the rich-person-in-disguise test as grand romance, but the person being tested is never told they are being graded; passing a test you never agreed to is not proof you are loved, it is proof you were being watched.
  Tema AS: night-shift diner tip culture (drives Nora's frugality, the job-offer scene, and the parking lane as their only private space); cash day-rate handyman work culture (Wyatt's pay envelope as physical proof of his fake life, and the reason the big-job offer endangers his cover).
  PLOT DNA: 1) motor konflik: rahasia identitas yang sengaja dipelihara plus persetujuan yang tidak pernah diminta | 2) relasi: pasangan tinggal bersama belum menikah | 3) titik balik: asisten pribadi memanggil nama lengkap dan jabatan asli Wyatt di depan Nora, ID perusahaan meluncur dan berhenti di kaki Nora | 4) resolusi: lamaran ditolak, hubungan diakhiri oleh pihak yang selama ini diuji | 5) nada: quiet-drama menuju bittersweet-firm | 6) hook: tangan menyembunyikan kotak cincin di bawah papan lantai tepat sebelum pintu terbuka
  Soft-limit elements used: kesulitan uang berupa hidup pas-pasan (motor pendukung, tanpa angka/nasihat keuangan); rasa dikhianati dalam relasi (perjalanan emosi tuntas di S05 lewat keputusan sadar Nora, bukan dipajang tanpa resolusi).
  Karakter berulang (jika serial): none.
  Summary: Wyatt menyamar delapan bulan sebagai tukang reparasi untuk menguji ketulusan Nora tanpa persetujuannya, identitas aslinya terbongkar oleh asistennya di lorong parkir diner, dan saat ia melamar dengan cincin yang sudah lama disiapkan, Nora menolak karena ujian diam-diam itu adalah bentuk kontrol, bukan cinta, lalu pergi dengan tenang.

EP024 — The Key Sewn Into a Stranger's Jacket
  Logline: Deacon Winslow, a former dockworker now sleeping on a city park bench, wants to save his late father's storage unit before it is cut open at dawn, but he has neither the key nor proof of standing, and finds both sewn into the lining of a jacket handed to him by a stranger who refuses to give his name.
  Creator insight: Among American blue-collar men a debt of gratitude is almost never repaid in money and almost never repaid in public; the one repaying it walks off before he can be thanked, because being thanked would turn a settlement into a favor.
  Tema AS: budaya self-storage dan lelang unit menunggak (memberi cerita tenggat keras pagi itu); budaya kerja pelabuhan dan solidaritas antar buruh lama (alasan Everett tahu siapa Deacon tanpa bertanya).
  PLOT DNA: 1) motor konflik: waktu (tenggat penyitaan) + warisan yang tersembunyi | 2) relasi: mantan rekan kerja lintas generasi yang tidak saling mengenali | 3) titik balik: jahitan saku dalam jaket robek dan kunci gembok bertag nomor unit jatuh ke telapak tangan Deacon, nomornya sama dengan nomor di surat peringatan | 4) resolusi: hak milik diselamatkan tepat waktu, tapi penolongnya sengaja tidak pernah muncul lagi | 5) nada: tense-urgent menuju quiet-warm | 6) hook: cold open flash-forward, gunting gembok berhenti di udara
  Soft-limit elements used: tunawisma (kondisi awal Deacon, resolusi manusiawi); tekanan uang berupa akun gudang menunggak (motor tenggat, tanpa angka/nasihat); duka atas ayah yang meninggal (selesai lewat aksi di S05). Kontak fisik antar tokoh: none.
  Karakter berulang (jika serial): none.
  Summary: Deacon menerima jaket dari orang asing yang menolak menyebut nama, menemukan kunci gembok unit gudang ayahnya dijahit di dalam lapisannya, menyelamatkan unit itu dari penyitaan tepat sebelum gemboknya dipotong, lalu tahu lewat catatan tanpa tanda tangan bahwa si pemberi sedang membayar utang budi lama kepada ayahnya di pelabuhan.

EP023 — The Package Marked Fragile
  Logline: A small-town delivery driver holds back a certified envelope meant for the family of the town's star quarterback on championship morning, because he once opened an identical letter at the worst possible moment and lost everything that mattered that day.
  Creator insight: Protecting someone's moment is sometimes more urgent than being technically on time — timing can matter more than the delivery itself.
  Tema AS: Budaya "Friday Night Lights" kota kecil Amerika (football SMA sebagai identitas komunitas) dan budaya kurir pengiriman lokal yang mengenal riwayat setiap keluarga di kotanya.
  PLOT DNA: 1) motor konflik: menahan pengiriman surat penting vs tekanan atasan untuk segera menuntaskan rute | 2) relasi: kurir (mantan pemain yang gagal) vs keluarga quarterback SMA saat ini | 3) titik balik: atasan mengancam memecatnya karena paket belum ditandatangani padahal sudah lewat jam kerja | 4) resolusi: ia antar sendiri tepat setelah peluit akhir pertandingan berbunyi, lalu mengaku alasan penundaannya | 5) nada: hangat, tegang secukupnya, berakhir lega | 6) hook: kurir berbalik pergi dari depan pintu tanpa mengetuk, padahal paket sudah ada di tangannya
  Soft-limit elements used: none
  Karakter berulang: none
  Summary: Seorang kurir kota kecil sengaja menahan surat penting milik keluarga quarterback SMA pada hari pertandingan besar karena trauma masa lalu, lalu mengantarnya sendiri tepat setelah laga usai — dan surat itu ternyata kabar beasiswa yang datang di waktu yang sempurna.

EP022 — The Blanket She Mended in Silence
  Logline: Harlan Sutton, a veteran widower, wants his late wife's wool blanket back, but wrongly accuses his neighbor Marisol of stealing it from a laundromat, and must face his own shame when he learns she was quietly mending it.
  Creator insight: The people we're quickest to suspect are often the ones quietly taking the most care of what we have — suspicion says more about our own fear than about them.
  Tema AS: coin laundromat culture (drives the missing-property plot and the wrongful accusation).
  PLOT DNA: 1) motor konflik: misunderstanding (missing property leads to wrongful accusation) | 2) relasi: neighbors (strangers living nearby) | 3) titik balik: Harlan finds Marisol mending his torn wool blanket with a floral stitch pattern identical to his late wife's | 4) resolusi: quiet reconciliation, wordless respect | 5) nada: quiet-drama to bittersweet-warm | 6) hook: sudden action, Harlan opens the washer and finds the blanket gone
  Soft-limit elements used: duka kehilangan pasangan (grief for late wife, resolved through action at S05, no empty emotional display).
  Karakter berulang (jika serial): none.
  Summary: Harlan salah menuduh tetangganya Marisol mencuri selimut wolnya dari laundromat, lalu tersadar gadis itu diam-diam menjahitnya dengan pola bordir bunga persis gaya mendiang istrinya, berakhir dengan rekonsiliasi diam penuh hormat.

EP021 — The Landlord Who Chased the Dog
  Logline: Boone Halloway, a rigid apartment landlord who just taped a final rent notice on tenant Wren Prescott's door, must chase her runaway dog Dodger into busy traffic, accidentally reopening a grief he's never spoken about.
  Creator insight: People who are toughest about rules are often guarding themselves from a loss they've never talked about — once you know why, the hardness reads as human, not cruel.
  Tema AS: small on-site-landlord apartment complex culture (drives the tenant-landlord conflict); urban leash-law/street-safety culture (drives the dog-escape danger).
  PLOT DNA: 1) motor konflik: sudden physical danger (dog escapes into traffic) exposing underlying rent pressure | 2) relasi: tenant-landlord | 3) titik balik: Boone catches the dog just before impact and is struck by the memory of his own dog's cracked collar tag "Chief" | 4) resolusi: rent grace period + new collar for Dodger, with Chief's old tag looped on in tribute | 5) nada: tense-relief menuju quiet-warm | 6) hook: sudden physical action (dog breaks loose, truck passes)
  Soft-limit elements used: kesulitan uang (telat sewa, tanpa angka/nasihat); bahaya fisik ringan tak disengaja pada hewan (S03, R10 aktif, tanpa cedera terlihat).
  Karakter berulang (jika serial): none.
  Summary: Boone menempel surat peringatan sewa di pintu Wren, lalu menyelamatkan anjingnya dari tabrakan dan tanpa sengaja membuka kembali kesedihannya sendiri, berakhir dengan kelonggaran sewa dan collar baru bertatahkan tag anjingnya yang telah tiada.

EP001 — The Photo in Her Wallet

  Soft-limit elements used: kesulitan uang (medical/ER urgency) — motor cerita, resolusi manusiawi, tanpa angka/nasihat keuangan ke penonton.
  Karakter berulang: none (episode standalone).
  Summary: Teller credit union yang taat aturan memilih jalur eskalasi resmi—bukan melanggar aturan—untuk membantu ibu panik menarik dana darurat bagi anaknya yang sakit.
